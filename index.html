How to play

Use the workshop controls to heat the forge and craft your swords.

heatForge(20)
Add 20 heat, up to 100.
makeSword()
Use 30 heat to make one sword.
resetForge()
Return to 20 heat and zero swords.

Add heat using positive whole numbers. The forge can reach a maximum of 100 heat.

Know your forge

  • 0–29Too cold to craft.
  • 30–69Ready to forge.
  • 70–100Roaring fire. Keep crafting!

Use positive whole-number heating amounts. Maximum forge heat is 100.
